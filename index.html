Fast Logging

Log Meals & Symptoms in Seconds

IBS and gut-health tracking usually fails because it's tedious — most people quit within a week. Darmi cuts down the typing: it automatically learns the foods you eat most and surfaces them as one-tap Quick Picks, so your regular meals become a couple of taps. You can still type anything that's new — there's just no food database to wade through, and no account to create.

  • Type anything new in seconds — no bloated food database to search; swipe to delete anything you got wrong
  • Encouraging feedback when you keep a logging streak going — the habit is what makes the patterns show up
  • Track symptoms, stool type (Bristol Stool Scale) and stress in one quick flow — suitable for IBS-C, IBS-D and IBS-M
  • No account required — no sign-up, no login, no server; works offline and your data stays on your device
02
Trigger Identification

Avoid Triggers. Find Your Safe Foods.

IBS symptoms often show up hours — sometimes days — after the food that caused them, which is exactly why triggers are so hard to spot by memory alone. So Darmi compares whole days: how often a food shows up on the days you had symptoms, against how often it shows up on the days you didn't. A food has to keep appearing on the bad days to surface as a finding. When you want the detail on one particular episode, opening that symptom shows what you ate in the six hours before it started.

  • Compares symptom days against symptom-free days to rank likely triggers
  • Surfaces recurring patterns, not just single incidents — with a six-hour lookback on any individual symptom
  • Helps distinguish likely trigger foods from your genuinely safe foods
03
Pattern Insights

Understand Your Body's Unique Patterns

Everyone's IBS is different — what triggers one person does nothing to another. Darmi's insights track how your symptoms trend over a rolling 7-day window, so you can see whether a change you made (a new food, less stress, a new routine) is actually working, not just guess. Pro widens that view to 14 and 30 days.

  • Symptom trend tracking over 7 days, extending to 14 and 30 days with Pro
  • Key Findings and Tolerated Foods surfaced automatically — no spreadsheet required; the full set unlocks with Pro
  • A calendar month view of everything you've logged, at a glance
  • Built to reveal your patterns, not generic IBS advice

Bring Better Data to Your Doctor

"It's been bad lately" isn't something a doctor can act on. Darmi keeps a structured, chronological history of everything you've logged — exportable and ready to share, so your next appointment starts with real data instead of a foggy recap.
